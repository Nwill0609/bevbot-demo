#1 GUAVA — BREAKOUT TREND OF THE YEAR
• Social media mentions: +340% year-over-year
• TikTok #GuavaSlush videos: 2.8 billion combined views
• Leading format: bubble tea, craft soda, agua fresca
• Profile: tropical, sweet-musky, pink hue — highly photogenic
• Key demographic: Gen Z, 18–28, urban markets
• Bubble tea chains report guava as fastest-selling new SKU in 2025
• Recommendation: HIGH PRIORITY — source now before price increases

#2 YUZU — PREMIUM CAFÉ ESSENTIAL
• Menu presence: +180% in premium café and restaurant menus
• Price point: commands 20–30% premium over standard citrus
• Leading format: cold brew flavor, sparkling water, cocktail mixer
• Profile: Japanese citrus, aromatic, tart, pairs well with honey and ginger
• Key demographic: Millennial, health-conscious, premium-spenders
• Import supply tightening — price expected to rise 15% in Q1 2026
• Recommendation: STOCK UP — limited seasonal availability

#3 HIBISCUS — HEALTH & WELLNESS DRIVER
• Consumer interest: +220% driven by antioxidant and wellness messaging
• Leading format: agua fresca, kombucha, herbal iced tea
• Profile: floral, tart, earthy, ruby red color (high visual appeal)
• Key demographic: health-conscious consumers, 25–45
• Works across hot and cold formats, strong year-round demand
• Recommendation: CONSISTENT PERFORMER — maintain steady inventory
